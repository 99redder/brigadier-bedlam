# v0.22 individual take-cover orders

A living regular soldier can enter/leave cover through a rebindable action (C standard, K mirrored) or the selected-unit button. It is allowed during deployment and persists after deselection. Entry stops movement and clears pursuit, demolition and formation orders. Covered soldiers reject move/bridge commands and are excluded from squad movement. Leaving cover holds position until a new movement order. Entry is unavailable in water or brawls, and recruits cannot use it.

A lower profile narrows rifle collision distance from 42 to 12 units and adds 16 degrees of incoming rifle aim spread. This uses the existing seeded projectile geometry, not an additional dodge coin flip. At close range that spread translates into less physical error, making closing the distance a counter. Explosives, fire, sniper powers and contact attacks bypass this stance's rifle protection. Terrain cover shielding remains independent. Ranged cooldowns are doubled, including manual rockets. Existing positive reload time doubles on entry once, repeated active orders do not multiply it, and exit does not shorten it.

A shortened soldier silhouette, turquoise ring/shield, raised IN COVER label, roster status, explanatory selected-unit copy and a Leave cover button make the state visible. Brawls/death/revival clear it. Settings migrate old saves by choosing a free key, preserving custom bindings.

174 tests pass. New coverage checks individual/group movement locks, exit and movement recovery, doubled reloads and anti-bypass behavior, manual rockets, a seeded hit-rate comparison and close-range counter, lethal explosive/melee counters, deployment, water/downed/recruit exclusions, deterministic replay and saved control migration. Browser QA exercised the actual C key and button toggles, confirmed the movement-rejection log and inspected the silhouette/status and footer controls. The ordinary AI remains unchanged and does not automatically use the new player stance.
